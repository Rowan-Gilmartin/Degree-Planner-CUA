CUA Degree Planner 
Problem. When students are trying to double major or add minors to their degrees it can be difficult to plan all the classes they need to take. 
Solution. This AI will search the CUA academic website and find what classes or needed for what major and what semster they are offered in. 
Target user. Students are CUA who are attempting to get double majors, minors, or 4+1 programs.
Success criteria. 1. The AI gives accurate course planning info. 2. The AI can plan create the plan within ten minutes. 3. The students that use it find it helpful, and after using the AI feel more confident in approaching their degree plan
